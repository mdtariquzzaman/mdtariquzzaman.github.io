# Agent instructions — academic website

## Working agreement

- Follow the user’s latest instructions. Keep durable design and workflow decisions here; do not duplicate biographies, publication records, metrics, or personal favorites.
- Use the PDF and LaTeX CV in `files/cv/` for factual records, together with approved sources in `content/` and explicit user corrections. Do not invent dates, interests, claims, or publication status. Preserve author order and distinguish accepted work from preprints. Never call an arXiv-only paper “accepted”, in news, badges, or copy: it is a preprint. `is_preprint()` in the builder suppresses the Accepted badge and fails the build if a news entry calls a preprint accepted.
- Maintain this guide in place. Do not create additional Markdown guides unless requested.
- Keep work inside this project. Verify the Git root before staging; never stage the parent home directory. Do not push or publish unless requested.
- `publication_input_iut_website.csv` is the import file for the IUT faculty publications profile. Keep its `Type,Date,Info` header, use ISO dates, preserve approved author order, and format `Info` as a complete readable citation with the paper title, venue or repository, year, and a final canonical URL to the external publication record. Classify every arXiv-only preprint as `Others`; an arXiv-hosted copy of a work with an approved workshop or conference venue keeps that venue classification. Never use the personal website or IUT profile URL as the citation link. Validate CSV quoting and confirm that every `Info` containing arXiv has `Type` set to `Others` before importing it.

## Design philosophy

Create a fun, nerdy academic portfolio with a coherent GBA-era RPG menu style. Home, Publications, the CV webpage, and Personal share the same visual world while helping visitors identify the researcher, understand his work, find outputs, and make contact.

- Keep authentic GBA menu construction with quiet pixel outlines, stepped corners, and shallow inset edges. Use a morning-paper day palette: lavender backdrop `#e8e5ed`, paper panels `#fff9ee`, ink `#303548`, and coral-brown accent `#884f46`. Night uses indigo backdrop `#181d30`, panels `#262c43`, light paper text, and coral accent `#e7b3a4`. Carry the same frame language through all pages, navigation, drawers, and footer. Use the paired minimal pixel-sky SVG backdrops; do not restore a green world, checker grid, arcade neon, CRT effects, or large floating shadows.
- Use self-hosted Pixel Operator Mono for mixed-case pixel headings, menu labels, and controls, and IBM Plex Mono for readable body copy and long metadata. No external font service is loaded. Preserve upstream font licenses. The bundled Literata files are **not** used by the web pages: keep `literata.ttf` and `literata-light.ttf` because `files/cv/tariq.tex` compiles the CV from them — never delete or re-encode those two — and retain the unreferenced `assets/fonts/literata.woff2`.
- Establish compact hierarchy through framed title strips, aligned record fields, typography, and spacing. Keep body copy and long titles readable, supporting metadata quieter, and paragraphs at comfortable line lengths. Copy may be shortened to remove repetition while preserving approved facts. Do not invent RPG levels, health bars, quests, achievements, or other personal claims.
- Keep the real portrait in the Home profile window and use a separate scene/social window beside it. Its original pixel still-life contains only a bookshelf with books, a window, a coffee cup on the sill, and plain wall/sky; no human, desk, laptop, language notes, or other props. Generate matching day/night versions with crisp GBA pixels and the same composition. The quiet Japanese influence is limited to wood, paper, and restraint, without garden scenery, spiritual symbols, or personal philosophy claims. Do not restore an “In the lab” card, CSS room, map hotspots, or “Tariq’s lab” branding. Visitors use familiar labels and direct navigation; do not gate content behind movement, a start screen, sound, or typing animation.
- Keep substantive content and navigation usable without JavaScript. Use JavaScript only to enhance theme controls, touch press feedback, publication drawers, figure carousels, and active-section highlighting.
- Make layouts responsive, preserve semantic reading order, and support keyboard focus, readable contrast, and reduced motion.
- Keep documentation and site content self-contained. Record asset sources and licenses in `readme.md`; do not depend on an external design-inspiration website.

## Source map

- `content/home.html`, `cv.html`, `personal.html`: approved page content and templates. The Home biography closes with the Systems and Software Lab membership line, linked to the lab site; the CV keeps the full appointment record.
- `content/publications.json`: paper metadata, author order, types, status, topics, and resources, plus each paper's `page` content. The site renders only `page.tldr`, `page.links`, and the figures (teaser, section and finding figures); the remaining section, finding, table, and video text is kept as source data. `award_url` links a named award to its official announcement on both the Publications list and paper page. `page.links` adds extra buttons to the entry.
- `content/scholar-metrics.json`: supplied/verified metrics and recorded date; `null` means unavailable.
- `content/personal.json`: ordered favorites with optional authors; `url` is the Wikipedia list-row destination. Keep any supplied artwork provenance in the data, but do not render copyrighted or unapproved artwork in the Personal lists.
- `content/teaching.json`: historical teaching records by term, not rendered in the CV. `content/news.json`: updates rendered on Home, with the newest three visible and older entries in “Earlier updates”.
- `publication_input_iut_website.csv`: import-ready IUT publication records; this is maintained separately from `content/publications.json` because the IUT profile has its own type/date/citation fields. Follow the CSV rules in `readme.md` when adding rows.
- `scripts/build.py`: shared HTML through `render()`, page metadata, publication groups/metrics, and favorite-list rendering. It also emits `404.html` (noindex), `sitemap.xml`, and `robots.txt`, and injects JSON-LD through `schemas` — the `Person` node on Home, and on Publications a `@graph` of that same node plus one `ScholarlyArticle` per paper (its `@id` is `publications.html#<id>`), with his authorship pointing at the Person's `@id` — plus Twitter card metadata on every page. These generated files are build output; edit the builder, not them.
- `assets/style.css`: design tokens, base typography, and layout. `assets/interactions.css`: buttons, drawers, carousel, section tones, responsive overrides, reduced motion, and print.
- `assets/app.js`: theme toggle (with reduced-motion-safe touch press feedback), publication drawers, figure carousels, and `.page-contents` scroll highlighting through `aria-current="location"`.
- `assets/fonts/`, `assets/licenses/`: self-hosted web fonts and retained font/icon licenses. Primary sources and provenance are listed in `readme.md`.
- `assets/illustrations/reading-corner-day.webp` and `reading-corner-night.webp`: original paired no-human bookshelf/window/coffee artwork, 1536x1024. `assets/illustrations/prompts.json` records the creation prompts and style reference. `assets/sky-day.svg` and `assets/sky-night.svg`: minimal coded pixel-sky backdrops. Keep illustration provenance here without adding another Markdown guide.
- `profile.jpg`: real portrait, 699x715 and about 150 KB. Retain its natural proportions and existing file quality in the profile window; do not turn it into a tall crop or upscale the binary. `files/cv/`: protected factual CV sources and downloadable PDF.
- `readme.md`: website documentation. `github-profile/readme.md`: separate prepared profile README for the `mdtariquzzaman/mdtariquzzaman` repository; keep consistent with approved content and publish only when requested. Its shields.io badges use green `425b46` for identity and venues, gold `a3864e` for interests and the award, and grey `68665d` for paper resources, all `style=flat-square`. Verify any new badge actually embeds its `logo=` slug, since shields silently drops unknown ones (LinkedIn's mark is unavailable, so that badge carries no logo). Keep blank lines between a paper's title, description, and badge row: a GitHub `.md` file collapses single newlines, which is what makes each badge row render as one row.
