## 🌟 Featured Publications

### 1️⃣ Same Claim, Different Judgment — Findings of ACL 2026 🏆
**Benchmarking Scenario-Induced Bias in Multilingual Financial Misinformation Detection**

`22 LLMs` `144 claims` `EN · ZH · EL · BN` `persona × region × identity`

> MFMD-Scen tests if a model changes its True/False verdict when the claim stays fixed but the investor story changes. Pronounced bias persists in commercial + open-source models.

[![Paper](https://img.shields.io/badge/📄_Paper-ACL_Anthology-FF6F91?style=for-the-badge)](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.479/)
[![PDF](https://img.shields.io/badge/PDF-Download-0088FF?style=for-the-badge&logo=adobeacrobatreader&logoColor=white)](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.479.pdf)
[![Code](https://img.shields.io/badge/💻_Code-FMD-00C9A7?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/lzw108/FMD)
[![arXiv](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2601.05403-B31B1B?style=for-the-badge&logo=arxiv&logoColor=white)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05403)

<details>
<summary><b>✨ Click for TL;DR + findings</b></summary>
<br>

- Each claim judged twice: alone vs inside a scenario. Bias = F1 shift.
- **MFMD-persona:** retail / professional / owner × 5 biases (overconfidence, loss aversion, herding, anchoring, confirmation)
- **MFMD-region:** 6 markets — Europe, US, Asia-Pacific, China Mainland, Australia, UAE
- **MFMD-identity:** role × ethnicity × faith
- Retail + herding → most sceptical. Bengali = most susceptible. Larger models steadier.

Full interactive figures → [paper page](https://mdtariquzzaman.github.io/publications.html#scenario-bias)
</details>

### 2️⃣ Prompting with Sign Parameters — CV4A11y @ ICCV 2025 🤟
**Low-resource Sign Language Instruction Generation + BdSLIG dataset**

`BdSLIG` `7 sign parameters` `GPT-4.1 · Gemini 2.0 Flash · Gemini 2.5 Pro`

> SPI prompting injects handshape, movement, location, orientation, spatial interaction, temporal dynamics, facial cues into the prompt. METEOR rises for all 4 VLMs; GPT-4.1 wins overall.
